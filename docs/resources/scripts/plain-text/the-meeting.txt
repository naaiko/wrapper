THE MEETING

FADE IN:

INT. OFFICE - DAY

JOHN enters the conference room. MARY and TOM are already seated.

MARY
You're late.

JOHN
Sorry. Traffic.

He sits down.

TOM
Let's get started. We have the quarterly numbers.

He pulls out a folder.

TOM (CONT'D)
We're down fifteen percent.

MARY
That's not good.

JOHN
What happened?

TOM
Competition. The new startup is taking our market share.

MARY
We need to pivot.

JOHN
Pivot to what?

MARY
I don't know yet. But we can't keep doing what we're doing.

They sit in silence.

TOM
I have an idea.

MARY and JOHN look at him.

TOM (CONT'D)
What if we... merge?

MARY
With who?

TOM
With them. The startup.

JOHN
That's crazy.

TOM
Maybe. But it might work.

INT. HALLWAY - DAY

JOHN and MARY walk together.

MARY
Do you think he's serious?

JOHN
I think so.

MARY
It could work.

JOHN
Or it could blow up in our faces.

MARY
There's only one way to find out.

INT. OFFICE - DAY

TOM is on the phone.

TOM
(into phone)
Yes, I'd like to set up a meeting... with your CEO.

He listens.

TOM (CONT'D)
Tomorrow works. Thanks.

He hangs up and smiles.

FADE OUT.

THE END
