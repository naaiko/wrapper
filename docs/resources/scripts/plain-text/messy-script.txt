MESSY SCRIPT

a script with inconsistent formatting


CAFE - DAY
(no INT/EXT)

Two people sit at a table. One is ALICE. The other is BOB.

ALICE
 So what did you think?

BOB
About what?

ALICE
The proposal. Did you read it?

BOB
  I skimmed it.

ALICE sighs.

ALICE
Bob, this is important.

BOB
    I know, I know. I'll read it tonight.

STREET
(still no INT/EXT, no time)

ALICE walks alone. She's thinking about the conversation.

Her phone rings.

ALICE
(answering)
Hello?

BOB (on phone)
I read it.

ALICE
And?

BOB
   It's good. Really good.

ALICE
(smiling)
Thanks.

PARK - LATER

They meet on a bench.

BOB
So when do we start?

ALICE
   Tomorrow?

BOB
Tomorrow.

They shake hands.

END
